## About CognitoByte
CognitoByte is an AI Automation Agency based in Islamabad, Pakistan. We help businesses worldwide automate repetitive tasks, build AI agents, and create custom chatbots. Our mission is to give every business the efficiency of a large enterprise team — without the headcount.

## Services

### n8n Automations
We design, build, and maintain custom n8n workflows. n8n is a powerful open-source automation tool that connects 300+ apps. We handle:
- App integrations (Gmail, Slack, Notion, Shopify, CRMs, databases, and more)
- Scheduled and event-triggered workflows
- Error handling, retries, and monitoring
- Documentation and handoff

### AI Agents
Autonomous AI systems that complete complex, multi-step tasks end-to-end:
- Lead research and qualification
- Document reading and data extraction
- Email drafting and scheduling
- Web browsing and form completion
- Customer follow-up sequences

### Chatbot Building
Custom AI chatbots trained on your business knowledge:
- Website chat widgets
- WhatsApp bots
- Slack/Teams bots
- Internal HR or support bots
- Trained on your docs, FAQs, policies

### Workflow Design
Strategic automation consulting before we build anything:
- Full process audit
- ROI analysis per automation
- Visual workflow maps
- Priority automation roadmap
